Mentoring guide · Future self

Meet the person you are becoming.

Give your present energy a shape. Create an image for your future self. Then stay with that future long enough to notice its feelings, qualities and everyday actions.

01 · Begin without comparison

This is a direction, not a verdict.

Your present self is not an unfinished version that needs to earn the future. Start by noticing what is here. Then let the future image show you something you care about.

Use this guide your way

Choose any section. Skip any prompt. “I do not know yet” is a complete answer. Colour, size and shape have no fixed meaning here. You decide what they mean.

The present and future images will appear one at a time. There is no need to compare them. The invitation is to spend some time inside the future image.

02 · Show your energy

Let today’s energy become a ball.

Use diameter as a metaphor. A small ball can mean quiet, contained or low energy. A large ball can mean expansive, activated or simply “a lot”. Your meaning can differ.

03 · Shape your future self

Choose the form that holds the future.

Pick a shape, colour and size. Follow what feels interesting. The shape does not need to be impressive. It only needs to help you stay with the future for a moment.

04 · Give the future words

Write from inside the future.

Stay with the image you made. Write in the present tense if that helps the future feel close. You can use direct words, fragments or a few concrete details.

05 · Add future actions

What does future me do?

Add abilities, habits, choices or ordinary moments. They can be ambitious or very small. “Speaks Japanese fluently” can sit beside “cares for a tiny plant”.

Your future actions will appear here.

06 · Stay with the feeling

Spend ninety seconds in the future.

Let the image stay on screen. You do not need to plan the route from here to there. Notice the future person’s pace, posture, surroundings and way of meeting the day.

07 · Your future self card

Keep the future somewhere visible.

Anything saved in sections 02–06 can appear here if you build a card. The card is a place to revisit the future feeling, not a contract or a measure of progress.
