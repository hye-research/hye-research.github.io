A game in six levels

Grid and Degrid

Visibilities land all over the uv plane, but the FFT wants a tidy grid. Learn how gridding moves them there, why it makes errors, and how the least-misfit kernel makes those errors tens to hundreds of times smaller. Then run it backwards: degridding.

Based on Ye, Gull, Tan & Nikolic 2020, MNRAS, 491, 1146. The W = 6 least-misfit kernel uses the published coefficients; other widths were optimised the same way for this game. Error curves are 1-D and illustrative.
