A game in seven levels

Build an Interferometer

Start with two antennas and one radio wave. Level by level, turn them into a telescope as big as your array: delays, correlation, visibilities, fringes, the uv plane and Earth rotation. Pass each lab, then answer the checkpoint to unlock the next level.

Made from the "Two antennas" slides of the talk Radio Interferometry in the Age of AI. Numbers are illustrative; formulas follow Thompson, Moran & Swenson.
