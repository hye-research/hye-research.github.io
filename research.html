Research

From imaging methodology to AI-enabled radio astronomy.

My work has a deep methodological root in radio interferometric imaging and a growing interdisciplinary branch in AI x radio astronomy.

Research positioning

I work on tools and workflows that make large radio surveys more precise, scalable, and scientifically useful. The central thread is turning complex interferometric data into reliable images and source populations that can support galaxy-evolution science.

Imaging methods

Least-misfit gridding and wide-field pipelines

Work on gridding, W-stacking, and sub-arcsecond LOFAR wide-field processing, with methods connected to production radio astronomy software.

AI x radio

Cross-resolution source identification

Machine-learning approaches for separating star-forming galaxies and AGN across radio survey regimes, including radio-quiet AGN recovery.

Infrastructure

Scientific computing and SKA-era workflows

Experience across SRCNet, SKA management and infrastructure, and collaborations involving high-performance data systems.
