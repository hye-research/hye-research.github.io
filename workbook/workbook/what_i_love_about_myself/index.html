Mapping Me

Things I like about myself

Loving something about yourself does not have to sound grand. It can be specific, quiet, funny, practical, uncertain, or still growing.

Specific is stronger

A specific appreciation can feel more believable than a huge statement. For example: I explain things clearly, I notice when a room feels off, I make people laugh, or I keep trying.

Notice: what is one specific thing you like about how you are?

Survival has skill in it

Some parts of you formed to get through hard conditions. They may deserve respect, care, and a better place to exist.

Notice: what helped you survive, adapt, or keep going?

What you keep choosing

The things you return to can show what matters to you. Maybe you keep choosing art, fairness, learning, comfort, honesty, beauty, people, or quiet.

Notice: what do you keep choosing, and what do you like about that part of you?

Very you, in a good way

Maybe there is a specific habit, preference, joke, routine, or way of doing things that feels very you. It does not have to make sense to everyone.

Notice: what is one very you thing you actually like?

What you do for people

When someone matters to you, what do you do? Maybe you bring food, help solve a problem, stay with them, or say the honest thing kindly.

Notice: what is one specific thing you have done for someone?

Not everything needs to be useful

You can like parts of yourself that are not about achievement: your laugh, softness, dramatic reactions, daydreaming, or tiny joys.

Notice: what part of you do you like, even if it is not useful or impressive?
