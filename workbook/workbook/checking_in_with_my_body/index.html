Mapping Me

Body signals as messages

Body check-ins are not about doing them perfectly. They are a way to notice signals that may arrive quietly, late, loudly, or all at once.

Signal volume changes

Some body signals whisper until they suddenly shout. Late noticing is a pattern to work with, not a character flaw.

Notice: which signals tend to arrive late?

Neutral counts

A check-in does not need to find a problem. Neutral, okay, blank, or hard to tell are all valid readings.

Notice: what does okay feel like, if anything?

Context shapes the body

Light, sound, hunger, temperature, pressure, people, transitions, and uncertainty can all change body messages.

Notice: what changed around you before the body changed?

Micro-needs matter

Small needs are easier to miss: water, posture, texture, bathroom, fresh air, movement, quiet, or a pause.

Notice: what small adjustment would make this moment 5 percent easier?

Body and brain talk

Your body can affect your thoughts, focus, and mood. Hunger, tension, noise, pain, or rest can change what your brain has available.

Notice: what might your body be telling your brain right now?

Permission to respond

A body signal does not have to be extreme before it deserves a response.

Notice: what would you do if the signal already counted?
