Neurodiversity

Mapping Me Workbook

A local-first workbook library for neurodivergent users. The framing is understanding, not correction.

Open workbook library

Included workbooks

Energy Map, Sensory Map, Learning Style Map, Discovering My Values, What I Love About Myself, Checking-In With My Body, and additional in-development tools.

Privacy model

Workbook notes save in the browser on the user's own device. They are not uploaded.

Visual language

Warm paper textures, bold collage shapes, clear silhouettes, and non-pathologizing language. The interface helps people notice patterns without forcing a fixed label.
