Students

A practical page for students and early-career researchers.

This page gathers possible project directions, mentoring expectations, and practical information for people interested in working with me.

Possible project areas

  • Radio interferometric imaging and calibration workflows
  • AI-assisted radio source classification
  • Galaxy evolution with radio and multi-wavelength survey data
  • Research software, reproducible pipelines, and scientific infrastructure

How I mentor

I value clear expectations, written structure, regular check-ins, and access-aware ways of working. Good research support should reduce hidden cognitive load, not add to it.

Before contacting me

Send a short note with what you want to work on, your current background, what kind of support you need, and any constraints on time, funding, or accessibility.
