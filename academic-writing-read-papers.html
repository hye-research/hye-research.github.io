Academic writing · 07

Read papers like a writer.

Good papers teach me how good papers are made.

Back to academic writing

01 · Build your judgement

I still recommend reading many papers.

Reading gives me examples of what works.

It also gives me examples of what feels difficult.

Over time, I develop a sense of clarity, pace, and useful detail.

This judgement helps me write. It also helps me evaluate suggestions from AI.

02 · Notice the construction

I ask how the paper is guiding me.

  • When does the research question become clear?
  • Where does the evidence appear?
  • How much context is enough?
  • Which paragraph changes the direction?
  • Where do I become lost?
  • Which references carry the argument?

I am not only reading the result. I am watching the writing decisions.

03 · Make a small map

I sometimes describe the job of each paragraph.

One paragraph creates the problem.

One narrows the scope.

One explains the method.

One shows the evidence.

This small map gives me a wider view. I can borrow the logic without copying the language.

04 · Direct the tool

I want the wider view before I ask AI for help.

If I know what good writing is doing, I can give better instructions.

I can ask for a clearer transition. I can ask for less detail. I can ask whether the main point arrives too late.

I do not need to accept the first structure the tool gives me.

I still need to write. Reading and writing build the judgement together.
